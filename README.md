# Needlfy

A small, fast Spotify player for Windows and macOS, with a spinning-record player and a notch player that hangs from the top of your screen.

## Run it

**Windows:** double-click the downloaded `needlfy-windows-x64.exe`; there's nothing to install. Needlfy isn't code-signed, so:

- your browser may say it "isn't commonly downloaded": choose **Keep**;
- Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**macOS:** open the downloaded zip and drag **Needlfy** into Applications. Needlfy isn't notarized, so the first time:

1. Open Needlfy. macOS says it can't check it for malicious software; click **Done**.
2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Needlfy.

You only do this once. After that Needlfy opens normally, and updates install themselves.

## Updates

Needlfy checks for new versions on its own. When there is one, click **Update** in its title bar: it downloads the new version, checks it, restarts, and removes the old one.
